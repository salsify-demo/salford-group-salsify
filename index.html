Tillage and application, dealer by dealer

Hundreds of dealers. One source for every machine.

Salford designs and builds tillage, seeding and application equipment under the Salford, BBI, Valmar and AerWay names, sold through a dealer network across the US and Canada. Every dealer site, spec sheet and parts lookup depends on the same product content. This page is a short look at how Salsify keeps that content consistent from factory to dealer lot.

Dealers sell with Salford's content, or without it

A grower researches a vertical tillage tool online, on a dealer site, before they ever talk to a salesperson. What a product change looks like with Salsify

1. Change onceA product manager updates a spec, image or description in Salsify, or it flows in from engineering or the ERP.
2. WorkflowThe right approvers sign off in their queue. Rules apply automatically.
3. ValidateReadiness reports check the record against every destination's current spec and image rules.
4. PublishOne click sends the correct format to dealer sites, listing platforms and salfordgroup.com.
5. MeasurePage grades and compliance scores come back into Salsify so the team sees what landed.
